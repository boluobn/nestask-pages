NesTask Privacy Policy

简体中文

Developer name: boluobn
Effective date: 2026-11-02.

This policy explains information handling for task files, purchases, optional agent connections, and this website.

1. Tasks and workspaces

Tasks, descriptions, progress, labels, and attachments are stored in your chosen workspace: iCloud Drive by default, or a folder on your Mac, an external drive, or a network volume. NesTask does not provide a developer-hosted task-sync server. iCloud syncing follows Apple’s Privacy Policy; other storage providers handle their own services.

Metadata uses ordinary JSON and attachments are files. NesTask does not apply app-level encryption to the workspace. Disk encryption, sharing, and backup protection depend on your system and service settings. Appearance, language, workspace location, and folder-access settings are stored locally.

2. Purchases and RevenueCat

NesTask uses Apple in-app purchases and RevenueCat to validate and restore purchases, determine entitlements, and provide purchase analytics. The app contacts RevenueCat at startup, periodic entitlement refreshes, and purchase or restore actions, not only when you pay.

RevenueCat processes an automatically generated anonymous app user ID, receipts or transactions, purchase history, entitlement status, last connection time, and device or app technical information. An anonymous ID does not mean no records are created or that they can never be associated with you. RevenueCat says IP addresses may be used to infer country and then discarded. Apple handles payment; RevenueCat does not collect payment-card details.

NesTask’s purchase-validation feature does not send task content, attachments, or workspace paths to RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes end-user data for the developer and may process data outside your region. See RevenueCat’s privacy information.

3. Optional agent connections

Connections are off by default. Once enabled and approved, the HTTP interface listens only on 127.0.0.1 on your Mac and provides task, progress, label, and permitted attachment access. Read-only, editor, and full-control permissions allow different operations.

A local interface does not keep all later processing local. Clients or model providers may send content to the cloud. Cursor’s OAuth callback may also pass through cursor.com with the authorization code, state, and iss; token exchange remains local and uses PKCE. Third-party processing depends on their services and your settings.

You can reduce permissions, revoke a client, or disable the service. Revocation stops future access. It does not delete third-party copies, undo completed actions, or automatically clear local authorization records and logs.

4. Local connection records

Authorization records, operation history, and encrypted recovery journals are stored on your Mac, outside the synced workspace. Journals may contain call arguments, planned fields, and results, including task titles, notes, progress, and attachment-upload content. They are not the same as limited operation history and are not content-free.

The current implementation applies a 30-day, 5,000-entry trimming window to operation history. New writes trigger cleanup of ordinary terminal journal records older than 7 days. Pending records and unknown outcomes are excluded. This is not a guarantee that every record is deleted immediately on expiry.

The Claude desktop extension also keeps its client ID and refresh token in a local file protected by file permissions. Uninstalling the extension does not automatically remove that file or revoke authorization in NesTask.

5. Deletion, support messages, and requests

Deleting a task normally moves its folder to the Trash. Deleting a label marks it as deleted; definitions and associations may remain. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove workspaces, purchase records, or subscriptions. Manage backups and copies held by sync providers or agents separately.

When you email support, the developer receives your address, message, and files you choose to provide to help resolve the issue. Purchase checks may need a transaction ID, which can associate the purchase record with that request. Do not send passwords, tokens, or unrelated private content.

Purchase-related records are retained while needed to provide and restore entitlements, check transactions, and meet necessary legal obligations. Support messages are retained while needed to resolve the issue and necessary follow-up, not for unrelated marketing. You can request access, correction, or deletion through support. We handle these requests manually. We first verify the connection between the request and the records, then address records we control and deletion requests we can make to service providers. If some information must be retained, we explain why. This does not promise simultaneous removal of copies lawfully retained by Apple or other providers, or copies you keep yourself.

Deleting a RevenueCat customer does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove Apple’s transaction records. Use Apple’s controls to cancel subscriptions.

6. This website and contact

These are static pages without added analytics scripts, ads, third-party fonts, or forms. When served through GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security whether signed in or not. See its Pages documentation and Privacy Statement. Linked websites follow their providers’ data practices.

Policy changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Send privacy questions and data requests through the contact address on the Support page.
