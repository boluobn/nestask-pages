Privacy Policy

Where are my tasks?

The default workspace is in your iCloud Drive. You can also choose a folder on your Mac, an external drive, or a network volume. Check the current workspace in the app’s settings. Switching workspaces does not delete the previous folder. If tasks seem missing, first check that the original location is selected.

Apple handles iCloud syncing. Check download and sync status in Finder if syncing has not finished. Keep a separate backup of important files.

Can I recover deleted data?

Deleting a task normally moves its folder and contents to the system Trash. You can inspect and restore them in Finder before emptying it. If deletion fails, follow the app’s message. Uninstalling NesTask does not automatically delete your workspace. Deleting a label marks it as deleted rather than erasing its definition and associations.

Purchases, restoration, and refunds

If you paid but access is missing, check that the App Store uses the Apple Account used for the purchase, then choose Settings → About NesTask → Restore Purchases. Purchases and entitlement checks need an internet connection and use RevenueCat. Contact support if the issue continues.

See Apple’s refund guidance for refund requests and Apple’s cancellation guidance for auto-renewing subscriptions. The purchase screen provides product, price, and renewal terms.

Connect or disconnect an agent

Enable connections in Settings › Agent Connections, follow the instructions to add a local client, and choose its access level in NesTask. NesTask must remain running with the authorized workspace open.

You can reduce permissions, revoke a client, or disable the service. Revocation stops future access; it does not clear local records and logs, remove content already received by a client, or undo completed actions. Agents may send content to cloud services, so review their privacy settings. Do not expose the local service through a tunnel or paste tokens into chats.
